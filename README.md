# Yulei's Machine Shop

Hi and welcome to my portfolio. I am no UIUX designer, but it was pretty fun playing with my original workshop model (in Fusion360, one of the only recent applications of my FRC MechE/ design team experiences), then rendering the model again by battling Blender. 

There have been two total iterations of the machine shop model, the first loosely based on my middle school (FTC) then high school robotics team (FRC) workshop, where I spent the majority of my time (it feels like), and most definitely where I lost some hearing in one ear from metalworking machinery... (thus if you know me, you've probably heard me say "I can't hear shit" at least twice per conversation).
