# Yulei's Machine Shop

Hi and welcome to my portfolio. I am no UIUX designer, but it was pretty fun playing with my original workshop model (in Fusion360, one of the only recent applications of my FRC MechE/ design team experiences), then rendering the model again by battling Blender. 

There have been two total iterations of the machine shop model, the first loosely based on my middle school (FTC) then high school robotics team (FRC) workshop. It was where I began to love robotics, where I spent the majority of my time (it feels like) bumming around in the stock storage loft above the machine shop for hours (inhaling sawdust, of course), and most definitely where I lost some hearing in one ear from metalworking machinery... (thus if you know me, you've probably heard me say "I can't hear shit" at least twice per conversation). Despite having to iterate a bit on the original design, I've been hoping to maintain the feeling of being in the machine shop, even in this (hopefully) slightly more professional seeing iteration.
